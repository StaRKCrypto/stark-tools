Privacy

This notice covers stark-bot.com, the static StaRK Bots site.

Updated 12 October 2026

Not financial advice. $STARKBOT is not issued. The contract address on the home page reads “Coming at launch” until it is published.

Who this is

The site is published by StaRKCrypto for StaRK Bots. Contact ceo@stark-bot.com. This page does not list a postal address because none is published in the project.

What the marketing pages collect

The home page, this page, and the terms page are static files. They do not create an account, do not run an analytics tag, and do not ask you to connect a wallet. The site operator does not receive your IP address in an application log we control; the host (currently the host that serves stark-bot.com) may keep ordinary server logs.

Paper desks

The paper desks under Apps save a trial timer in your browser’s localStorage under keys that start with starkbots_trial_v1. That timer is the calendar day and a start time for a five-minute paper session. It stays on your device. Clearing site data removes it. Those desks do not ask for a private key.

Fonts

IBM Plex is loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Requesting those files tells Google your browser asked for the font. Their handling of that request is covered by Google’s own policy.

Cookies

These pages do not set a cookie of their own. A font host or your browser may still store data under its own rules.

Children

The site is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it. The contract address, if one is published later, is not personal data and will appear on the home page.

Contact

Email ceo@stark-bot.com for a privacy question. Brand account: @StaRKCryptoBots. Personal account: @_Starkcrypto.
